How it works

          
From key to first video in three calls.

        

        
Media jobs run asynchronously, so a long Veo render never ties up your server. Chat models answer in the same request.

      

      

        
Step 1
Create a key
Sign up, claim your free credits, and copy one key that works for every model.

        
Step 2
Send a task
POST to /v1/tasks with a model and its input. Switching models means changing one string.

        
Step 3
Get the result
We call your callback_url when the file is ready, or you poll /v1/tasks/{id}.

app.py


from openai import OpenAI

client = OpenAI(
    base_url="https://api.easytoken.si/v1",
    api_key="et_live_...",
)

reply = client.chat.completions.create(
    model="claude-opus-5-5",
    messages=[{"role": "user", "content": "Write a 15s ad script"}],
)
print(reply.choices[0].message.content)

      

    

  


  

    

      

        
FAQ

        
Questions before you switch.

      

      

        
Are these the official models?
Yes. EasyToken routes your request to the model provider's own endpoint or an authorised host. The output is the same model you would get directly.

        
How can you be cheaper than the official API?
We buy capacity in volume across many customers and pass most of the discount on. Prices are listed per model, and you see the cost before each task runs.

        
What happens when a task fails?
Failed, timed-out and safety-filtered tasks are refunded to your balance automatically. You don't need to file a ticket.

        
Do credits expire?
No. Prepaid credits stay on your account until you use them.

        
Are there rate limits?
Every account starts with generous concurrency per model. If you need more for a launch, contact us and we raise it the same day.

        
Can I get an invoice for my company?
Yes. Add your company name and VAT number in billing settings and every top-up generates a proper invoice.
